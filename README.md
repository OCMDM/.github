# OCMDM
## Open Computer for Market Diversity Movement
### About

An international initiative advocating for a diverse, open and competitive computing market.

### Our vision

- Architectural diversity
- Independent manufacturers
- Open hardware standards
- OS diversity
- Specialized computing
- Market competition

### Our approach

One broader platform connecting specialized hardware and software subplatforms through shared standards and compatibility requirements.

### Current status

We're looking for enthusiasts, developers, engineers, researchers and potential manufacturers interested in helping shape the initiative.

### Get involved

Join our discussions, share your ideas, and help us build a more diverse computing industry.
